The solo build's direct cost was $260: about $220 of Claude subscriptions (Pro for the first two weeks, then Max 5x) plus a Google AI Pro plan for NotebookLM, with everything else on free tiers. That figure is striking but misleading in isolation: the dominant cost of any L&D project is labor, and here the practitioner's roughly 210 focused hours were uncosted, an approximate opportunity cost of about $10,500. The organizational estimate restores the full cost: roughly $190,000–$258,000 for a five-person team delivering the same scope to 200 participants. It is an approximate sample that shows the shape of the cost, not a number to commit to.

Salary estimates are fully loaded at an assumed 1.4 times base salary for benefits, taxes, and overhead, and use market rates for mid-to-senior L&D and engineering roles in major metros such as Singapore and Hong Kong. The multiplier is a planning assumption. In US data, benefits alone, legally required ones included, bring total compensation to about 1.43 times wages{{cite:bls-ecec-2026#private-benefits-30}}, so 1.4× roughly covers benefits and taxes and leaves out overhead. An adopting organization would substitute its own rates.

## Where the money goes

Labor accounts for about 80% of the organizational total, the sum of the five labor lines in the budget estimate. The full-stack developer is the single largest line item, because the custom platform is the most labor-intensive deliverable. An organization using an off-the-shelf LMS or authoring tool would eliminate that line but lose the interactive components (data dashboards, tokenizer playground, AI sandbox) that differentiate the program.
